Health & Safety Declaration
Please read the full declaration, confirm your agreement, and sign below.

Working at Height — Health & Safety Declaration

I, the undersigned, hereby declare and confirm the following:

  • I have attended and actively participated in the Working at Height safety training course as required under the Work Safety Regulations (Working at Height), 5766-2007.
  • I have been fully briefed on the potential hazards and risks associated with working at height, including fall risks, equipment failure, and environmental hazards.
  • I understand my obligations under the Work Safety Ordinance [New Version], 5730-1970, and the applicable Israeli safety regulations.
  • I am in good health and am not aware of any physical or medical condition that would prevent me from safely performing work at height.
  • I have been trained in the correct use, inspection, and maintenance of personal protective equipment (PPE), including harnesses, lanyards, and anchor points.
  • I understand the emergency procedures to follow in the event of a fall or accident at height, including rescue protocols.
  • I commit to conducting a risk assessment prior to any work at height activity and to never working at height alone when conditions present an unacceptable risk.
  • I agree to immediately report any unsafe conditions, equipment defects, or near-miss incidents to my supervisor or site safety officer.
  • I understand that this certificate is valid for two (2) years from the date of issue and that I am responsible for renewing it before expiry.
  • I acknowledge that this declaration forms part of my safety record and may be inspected by regulatory authorities or my employer.

I confirm that all information I have provided is accurate and complete. I understand that providing false information may result in the revocation of my certificate.

Your Signature *
