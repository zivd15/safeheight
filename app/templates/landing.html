Why SafeHeight?
100% Paperless.
Legally Defensible.
  • 🔐
    Cryptographic Verification
    Every certificate carries a unique SHA-256 hash. Any tampering instantly breaks verification.
  • 📲
    Magic Link & OTP Access
    Students access their portal via a single-use secure link — no passwords to forget.
  • ✍️
    Digital Health Declaration
    Students sign a legally binding health declaration on screen before certification is issued.
  • 🗄️
    Instant Public Verification
    Safety officers can verify any certificate in seconds — from any device, no login required.
  • ⏰
    Automatic 2-Year Renewal Alerts
    The system tracks expiry dates and flags renewals before they lapse.
